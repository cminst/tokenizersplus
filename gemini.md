### One Immediate Action Item for You

To get the **Unigram Baseline** (Day 1 task) without training a full SP model from scratch if you are pressed for time:

```python
import sentencepiece as spm
# Trains a Unigram model with 16k vocab
spm.SentencePieceTrainer.train(
    input='data/train.txt', 
    model_prefix='unigram_baseline', 
    vocab_size=16000, 
    model_type='unigram'
)

```
